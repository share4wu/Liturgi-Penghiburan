## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a sanctuary of dignity, solace, and warm reverence for memorial and funeral liturgies (*Ibadah Penghiburan* / *Bidston*). Designed for mobile-first congregational use during moments of profound emotion and quiet reflection, the experience minimizes sensory overwhelm, visual noise, and technical friction.

The visual direction marries **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Sacred Restraint**:
- **Tone:** Reverent, comforting, unhurried, and graceful.
- **Audience:** Grieving families, elder congregation members, officiants, and guests following along on mobile devices in low-lit chapels or sanctuaries.
- **Atmosphere:** Warm morning light filtered through linen, botanical quietude, and enduring remembrance. Crisp, highly legible typography takes precedence over ornamentation, while delicate warm stone outlines and soft metallic accents honor the solemnity of the occasion.

## Colors

The palette draws from natural stone, ancient parchment, brushed bronze, and quiet olive flora to establish an environment of deep emotional comfort and clarity.

- **Primary (`#A38048`):** Deep antique bronze gold. Used for liturgical section markers, reverent dividers, sacred monograms, and solemn focal accents.
- **Secondary (`#6B8E7B`):** Muted laurel sage. Symbolizes life, hope, and peace; deployed for responsive liturgical badges, musical cues, and tranquil status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C49F60`):** Soft luminous gold. Used sparingly for hover states, hymn verses emphasis, and warm decorative micro-accents.
- **Neutral Primary (`#2D3748`):** Deep charcoal slate. Replaces harsh true black to protect eye comfort while maintaining WCAG AAA contrast against cream foundations.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#596579`):** Soft slate charcoal for congregational responses, hymn composer credits, and Scripture references.
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster cream)
  - Section alternate / footer: `#F5F3EF` (Warm ecru)
  - Liturgy cards & prayer surfaces: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
  - Outlines & hairpins: `#E8E4DD` (Warm stone border)
  - Sage tint container: `#EAF0EC` (Soft eucalyptus wash)

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy accommodates multi-generational participation with an emphasis on clarity for attendees holding phones at waist level or arm's length.

- **Display & Headings:** `Playfair Display` provides a classical, commemorative dignity suitable for memorials, obituary headings, and sacred liturgical orders.
- **Liturgical Prose & Hymns:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` is tuned with wide tracking (`0.01em` to `0.02em`) and generous line heights (up to `32px` on body and hymn verses). This prevents line skipping when congregation members sing or follow responsive readings aloud.
- **Congregational Roles:** Speaker distinctions (e.g., *Pemimpin*, *Jemaat*, *Keluarga*) utilize `label-lg` transformed to uppercase with tracking `0.08em` in `#A38048` or `#6B8E7B`.
- **Scripture Quotations:** Rendered in `scripture-verse` (italicized `Playfair Display`) centered between fine rules or within padded inset margins.

## Layout & Spacing

The single-page liturgy employs a centered, single-column reading column on mobile that expands into a bounded, peaceful reading canvas on larger screens:
- **Maximum Content Width:** `720px` for the primary liturgy column to maintain optimal line-lengths (55–65 characters per line for hymns and prayers).
- **Mobile Viewport (<640px):** Single-column layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding, ensuring cards sit close to screen edges while preserving breathing room.
- **Tablet / Desktop (≥640px):** Liturgy container centers with `2.5rem` to `3rem` margins against the `#FAF9F6` atmospheric backdrop.
- **Step-by-Step Flow:** Vertical element spacing uses `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) between consecutive cards and `space-2xl` (`3.5rem`) between core liturgical divisions (e.g., *Ibadah Pembukaan*, *Firman Tuhan*, *Penutup & Berkat*).

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain a calm and organic spiritual environment, heavy drop shadows and artificial elevations are strictly avoided in favor of **low-contrast outlines, subtle tonal elevation, and ambient ground glow**:

- **Surface Card Level 1 (Liturgy Cards):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill resting on the `#FAF9F6` background, bordered by a single `1px solid #E8E4DD` rule. An ambient grounding tint (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 55, 72, 0.03)`) gives paper-like weight without artificial gloss.
- **Surface Level 2 (Active Liturgy Step / Current Hymn):** Highlighted with a delicate primary border (`1px solid #A38048`) and an elevated warm wash (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(163, 128, 72, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Modal / Family Tribute Notice):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a soft blur backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with a deep, diffused ambient shadow (`0 16px 36px -6px rgba(45, 55, 72, 0.08)`).
- **Dividers & Florals:** Centered hairline strokes (`1px #E8E4DD`) punctuated by delicate SVG botanical leaf or Latin cross motifs rendered in `#A38048`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates gentle curvature to soften structure and invoke comfort, avoiding harsh brutalist corners or excessively playful pill forms:

- **Base Cards & Modules:** `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for liturgical step containers, giving them an approachable, tablet-of-remembrance look.
- **Badges & Role Chips:** `rounded-lg` (1rem) for soft, pill-adjacent tags (*Jemaat*, *Liturgos*, *Pujian*).
- **Buttons & Action Triggers:** Standardized at `rounded` (0.5rem) to preserve structured dignity.
- **Photo Vignettes:** The portrait of the deceased uses an elegant arch-top silhouette (`border-radius: 999px 999px 0.5rem 0.5rem` or an oval cameo) framed by a 1px `#C49F60` stroke and a generous 8px white matte ring.

## Components

### Hero Memorial Banner
- **Structure:** Centered memorial photograph inside an arched or oval cameo frame with a thin gold accent border.
- **Content:** Full name of the departed, birth and resting dates, and an anchoring comforting Bible verse (e.g., 2 Timotius 4:7 or Mazmur 23:1) rendered in `scripture-verse`.

### Liturgical Order Cards (Step-by-Step Flow)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` card, 1px `#E8E4DD` perimeter border, `rounded-lg` (1rem), with internal padding of `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- **Header:** Sequence badge (e.g., "01. PANGGILAN BERIBADAH") set in `label-sm` bronze uppercase, accompanied by the liturgical leader role chip.
- **Leader / Congregation Interaction:**
  - *Pemimpin (P):* `#2D3748` regular weight text with a left gold accent marker.
  - *Jemaat (J):* `#2D3748` bold weight text tinted with a subtle `#FAF9F6` inset background block to prompt unanimous congregational response.

### Hymn & Lyric Blocks
- **Container:** Warm cream inset card with an optional audio play trigger for the accompaniment MIDI/organ track.
- **Title:** `headline-sm` with hymn source label (e.g., *Kidung Jemaat 453 / KJ 453:1-3*).
- **Lyrics:** `hymn-lyrics` specification with line breaks grouped into stanzas via `space-md` gaps. Stanzas feature bold lead numbers in `#A38048`.

### Responsive Badges & Role Chips
- **Liturgos / Pemimpin Chip:** Filled with `#EAF0EC`, text colored `#6B8E7B`, border `1px solid rgba(107, 142, 123, 0.25)`.
- **Semua / Jemaat Chip:** Filled with `#FAF9F6`, text colored `#A38048`, border `1px solid #E8E4DD`.
- **Paduan Suara / Solo Chip:** Filled with `#FFFFFF`, text colored `#596579`, border `1px solid #E8E4DD`.

### Action Buttons & Navigation
- **Primary Button (e.g., "Unduh Warta Liturgi PDF", "Kirim Ucapan / Belasungkawa"):**
  - Background `#A38048`, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle hover shift to `#8E6D38`.
  - Height 48px to accommodate finger taps under distress; font `label-lg`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button (e.g., "Putar Musik Pengiring"):**
  - Background transparent, border `1px solid #A38048`, text `#A38048`, hover background `rgba(163, 128, 72, 0.05)`.

### Form Fields (Condolence & Prayer Wall)
- **Input & Textarea:**
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E4DD`, radius `0.5rem`, text `#2D3748`, placeholder `#596579` with 60% opacity.
  - Focus state: border color `#A38048`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(163, 128, 72, 0.15)`. No harsh primary halos.

### Sacred Dividers
- Hairline horizontal lines in `#E8E4DD` centered with a botanical olive sprig or a minimalist cross glyph in `#A38048` to gracefully separate liturgical movements.
